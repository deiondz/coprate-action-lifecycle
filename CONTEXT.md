# Domain glossary

## Announcement

An exchange filing used as evidence. An announcement is never itself a corporate-action lifecycle.

## Corporate-action lifecycle

A persistent interpretation of one corporate action for one listed company, assembled from one or more announcements.

## Stage

A named point in an action-type-specific lifecycle. A later announcement does not advance a stage unless its evidence supports that transition.

## Change

A material state or structured-field difference introduced by an announcement.

## Confirmed date

A date stated by source evidence.

## Expected step

The stage that typically follows the current stage. It is guidance, not a confirmed outcome.

## Needs review

A lifecycle-matching outcome used when more than one existing lifecycle is a plausible target. The announcement is retained but not silently merged.
